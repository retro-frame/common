2. TRBV WORD LISTS
===============================================================================

TRBV specifies two separate word lists, one consisting of 4-letter words and
the other one consisting of 5-letter words.

Each word differs from all other words on at least two positions. Words with a
similar pronunciation have been avoided. However, a "phonetic distinctiveness"
like the [PGP WORD LIST] is not achieved.

3. TRBV PATTERN
===============================================================================

TRBV specifies two patterns which represent a sequence of 4-letter respectively
5-letter words from the two word lists 4word and 5word. As both word lists are
disjunct, a 4word-5word pattern is different from a 5word-4word pattern.

The hyphen-minus '-' character is used to separate two adjacent words.


3.1 32-bit pattern
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A 32-bit value is represented by four 4-letter words, i.e. the following
pattern:

  4word-4word-4word-4word

The first word is determined by the highest 8 bits of the value, followed by
the next 8 bits and so on. The resulting pattern has a length of 19 characters.


3.2 64-bit pattern
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A 64-bit value is represented by a mix of 4-letter words and 5-letter, three of
each, where the pattern depends on the highest 5 bits of the value:

  0x00:  4word-4word-4word-5word-4word-5word-5word
  0x01:  4word-4word-4word-5word-5word-4word-5word
  0x02:  4word-4word-4word-5word-5word-5word-4word
  0x03:  4word-4word-5word-4word-4word-5word-5word
  0x04:  4word-4word-5word-4word-5word-4word-5word
  0x05:  4word-4word-5word-4word-5word-5word-4word
  0x06:  4word-4word-5word-5word-4word-4word-5word
  0x07:  4word-4word-5word-5word-4word-5word-4word
  0x08:  4word-4word-5word-5word-5word-4word-4word
  0x09:  4word-5word-4word-4word-4word-5word-5word
  0x0A:  4word-5word-4word-4word-5word-4word-5word
  0x0B:  4word-5word-4word-4word-5word-5word-4word
  0x0C:  4word-5word-4word-5word-4word-4word-5word
  0x0D:  4word-5word-4word-5word-4word-5word-4word
  0x0E:  4word-5word-4word-5word-5word-4word-4word
  0x0F:  4word-5word-5word-4word-4word-4word-5word
  0x10:  4word-5word-5word-4word-4word-5word-4word
  0x11:  4word-5word-5word-4word-5word-4word-4word
  0x12:  4word-5word-5word-5word-4word-4word-4word
  0x13:  5word-4word-4word-4word-4word-5word-5word
  0x14:  5word-4word-4word-4word-5word-4word-5word
  0x15:  5word-4word-4word-4word-5word-5word-4word
  0x16:  5word-4word-4word-5word-4word-4word-5word
  0x17:  5word-4word-4word-5word-4word-5word-4word
  0x18:  5word-4word-4word-5word-5word-4word-4word
  0x19:  5word-4word-5word-4word-4word-4word-5word
  0x1A:  5word-4word-5word-4word-4word-5word-4word
  0x1B:  5word-4word-5word-4word-5word-4word-4word
  0x1C:  5word-4word-5word-5word-4word-4word-4word
  0x1D:  5word-5word-4word-4word-4word-4word-5word
  0x1E:  5word-5word-4word-4word-4word-5word-4word
  0x1F:  5word-5word-4word-4word-5word-4word-4word

The first word is determined by the highest 8 or 9 bits of the value after the
5 bits which determine the pattern (see above). The next word is determined by
the following 8 or 9 bits and so on. The resulting pattern has a length of 37
characters.

2. TRBV
===============================================================================


TRBV-64: 64 bit pattern (length=37):

  The bits for the pattern are taken from a 64 bit big endian value from
  highest bits. The bits for the words are taken from the value in order of
  the pattern from highest to lowest bits.

  Example: 0x0F1E2D3C4B5A6789

  ==> Pattern 0x001: 4word-4word-4word-5word-5word-4word-5word
  ==> 4word 0x0E3: TRIO
  ==> 4word 0x0C5: SIGN
  ==> 4word 0x0A7: PALM
  ==> 5word 0x112: MICRO
  ==> 5word 0x1AD: STICK
  ==> 4word 0x033: DIVE
  ==> 5word 0x189: SHARP

  TRIO-SIGN-PALM-MICRO-STEAM-DIVE-SHARP
